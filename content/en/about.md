I’m Tien-Dat Do. Rather than define myself with a list of labels, I want to introduce myself through three philosophical questions that I keep returning to. They help me make sense of my identity, my beginnings, and the direction I hope to take.

## Who am I?

I’m a student in the Faculty of Information Technology (FIT) at the University of Science, VNU HCMC. I’m especially interested in Theoretical Computer Science, Combinatorics, Optimization, Machine Learning, and Quantum Computing.

I’m shaped by the things I study, the people I meet, and the work I choose to do.

## Where did I come from?

I’m from Quy Nhon :)), a beautiful coastal city in Binh Dinh Province, Vietnam (now part of Gia Lai Province). It’s home to my family, the people who raised me, and many memories that helped shape who I am.

## Where am I going?

I don’t have a complete map. I want to keep learning and share what I learn along the way.
